Expertise

Public Procurement

We have rich experience in providing legal support in public procurement procedures, including:

  • preparation and submission of bids,
  • appeals against the tender documentation and the contracting authority’s decisions,
  • representation in administrative disputes relating to decisions of the State Commission,
  • consultation in the process of contracting.

In addition, the firm has significant experience in representing clients in court and arbitration proceedings arising from disputes over public contracts.

Significant areas in which we provide public procurement services include environmental protection, forestry, road planning and design, and energy.
